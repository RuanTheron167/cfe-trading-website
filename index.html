WHY IMPORTERS WORK WITH US

IMPORTING FROM CHINA SHOULDN'T FEEL LIKE A GAMBLE.

Most South African businesses trying to import from China run into the same three walls: they can't communicate directly with factories, they have no way to verify quality before the container leaves port, and once it lands they're on their own with customs, duties and clearing agents.

CFE Trading removes all three. We speak fluent Mandarin, so we negotiate directly with factory owners — not agents padding the price. We inspect goods on the ground before they ship. And we manage the entire journey from the factory floor in China to your door in South Africa, under one team, one process, and one point of contact.
